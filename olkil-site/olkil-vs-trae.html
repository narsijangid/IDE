Why compare OLKIL and Trae

AI IDEs differ less in the marketing and more in the feel: how quickly the editor responds, how much project context the AI actually uses, and how easy it is to review changes before they land. Those are the things worth testing yourself.

What you get with OLKIL

OLKIL is a professional desktop IDE with AI built into the workflow rather than bolted on. Version 1.3.11 installs on Windows 10 and later, and there is no plan to buy before you can start working on a real project.

  • Project-aware AI that respects your existing patterns
  • Fast navigation and editing in large workspaces
  • Clear, reviewable edit proposals
  • Free to download and keep using

How to decide

Take one real task from your backlog — a refactor with tests is ideal — and run it through both tools. Judge on how much of the output you keep, not on how impressive the first response looks.
