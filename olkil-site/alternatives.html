What to look for in an AI code editor

Most AI coding tools in 2026 sit somewhere on a spectrum: assistants that plug into an existing editor, and full AI-native IDEs that own the whole workflow. When you compare options, the questions that actually matter day to day are:

  • Does the AI understand your repository, or just the open file?
  • Can you install it and start working without a paid plan?
  • Is the editor itself fast on large workspaces?
  • Do proposed edits land as reviewable changes you control?
